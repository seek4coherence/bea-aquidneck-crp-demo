Why this matters on Aquidneck Island

Community facing story cards. Production copy would come from Cascadia narratives and your Communications team.

Land

Protect natural buffers

Shoreline habitat, wetlands, and open space help absorb storms and protect neighborhoods. Projects like Almy Pond restoration rebuild those living defenses.

Water

Guard drinking water and coasts

Easton Ponds serve roughly 35,000 year round residents plus seasonal population and Naval Station Newport. Dam and floodplain projects reduce saltwater intrusion and inland flooding risk.

People

Plan together as one island

Water does not stop at town lines. Aquidneck Resilience connects Newport, Middletown, Portsmouth, and Naval Station Newport through Working Groups and a shared Climate Resilience Plan.

How Aquidneck Resilience staff update this

Designed so routine progress updates do not need a developer. Edit a spreadsheet. Publish. The dashboard refreshes.

Content source of truth

  1. Cascadia and staff maintain an Excel or Google Sheet workbook (plus ArcGIS layers for maps).
  2. Each row is one action or project: theme, town, status, lead, funding, dates, progress, summary.
  3. Staff change a status or progress value in the sheet when a milestone moves.
  4. A lightweight publish step rebuilds the public dashboard from that sheet. No SaaS lock in required.
  5. Squarespace Personal Plan embeds or links the hosted dashboard. Your ArcGIS maps stay in ArcGIS.

This demo ships with data/actions.csv so evaluators can see the exact column model. Production can sync from Microsoft 365 Excel, Google Sheets, or a static CSV export.

Ask the plan Optional feature concept

A clearly labeled optional assistant that answers from published plan text only. Not included in the base $24,800 fee unless you request it as an add on.

This is a local demo mock. It searches the sample actions on this page only. No data leaves your browser.

Ethical use note: production answers would be grounded in Cascadia published content, with citations, human review for public release, and no use of client data to train public models.

Accessibility and audiences

  • Section 504 oriented structure: semantic headings, keyboard operable filters and table rows, visible focus, live region for details.
  • Color is not the only cue: status text labels sit beside chips; charts also appear as tables of counts in the KPI row.
  • Mobile responsive layout tested for phone width viewing.
  • Public view stays simple. Practitioner detail is one click deeper, matching the RFP dual audience goal.
